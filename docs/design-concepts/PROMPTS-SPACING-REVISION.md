# Homepage spacing revision: exact prompts

Generated 12 November 2026 with the built-in image generation tool following the owner's feedback that all four initial round-2 boards felt crowded. The owner subsequently approved the roomier white version and the numeric values in [DESIGN_FOUNDATIONS.md](../DESIGN_FOUNDATIONS.md). P1.01–P1.08 now use those values in the local React shell. The night version remains exploration only.

This revision replaces the full-page overview with an upper-page view at normal scale. Desktop and mobile are presented separately. The desktop product row now has three pieces; mobile retains two columns. Story, events, newsletter and footer continue below the shown viewport. These changes respond to the latest owner feedback and supersede the earlier full-page composition for these revision images only. The fixed identity, navigation and illustrative prices remain in place.

Both `01-minimal-shop.png` and `02-editorial-home.png` were attached to every generation as style references. All three final images were visually checked for wordmark accuracy, readable text, theme treatment and spacing. Prices shown are within the agreed range. The collapsed mobile menu represents the same five navigation destinations; it does not add a crowded horizontal link row.

The original shared style block starts each prompt unchanged, except the white desktop version removes its amber sentence. The mixed mobile comparison uses the original shared block and specifies a night view and an all-white view. No code or cloud resources were created during generation. The prompts below remain a historical record; named fonts and measured values in the current foundations document govern implementation.

## 05-home-night-spacious.png

```text
Use case: ui-mockup. High-fidelity website design board for NIGHT IN KAMPALA, a Ugandan fashion brand that reworks secondhand clothing with original hand-painted and screen-printed designs, sells one-of-one pieces to Uganda and international shoppers, and runs fashion events. Proposed interface, illustrative content only.
Board: wide landscape, neutral off-white ground, flat front-facing viewports, no perspective, no device hardware, no decorative scene. All UI text large, sharp and correctly spelled.
Identity: wordmark "NIGHT IN KAMPALA" set in a heavy condensed grotesque, all caps, tight tracking, always on one line on desktop. Headings in the same condensed face. Body and UI labels in a clean neutral grotesque. Short editorial sublines in a quiet serif.
Palette: paper white #FFFFFF, ink near-black #0B0B0C, fog grey #F1F1EF for product image fields, hairline grey #E2E2DF for borders. One accent only: warm streetlight amber #F2A93B, used sparingly for a single highlight per screen, never for body text or large fills.
Components: square corners, thin 1px borders, rectangular black buttons with white caps text, small rectangular labels "ONE OF ONE", "NEW", "SOLD". Thin-line icons for menu, search, account, bag. Market control "UG / UGX".
Navigation, identical on every board: "Shop", "Drops", "Our process", "Lookbook", "Events".
Garments: original photorealistic reworked pieces, washed black tees, blue denim, off-white camp shirts, olive workwear, with bold hand-painted white or black abstract brushwork (suns, wavy lines, linework faces, geometric blocks). Visible fabric texture, mended seams and patching. No recognisable brand logos.
People: young adult Black East African models, dignified contemporary fashion photography, natural skin texture.
Prices in Ugandan shillings between UGX 85,000 and UGX 220,000.
Avoid: gradients, glassmorphism, rounded SaaS cards, neon, nightclub imagery, skyline clichés, stock-photo smiles, fake reviews, impact statistics, invented dates or venues.
Tiny footer outside the UI: "DESIGN CONCEPT · ILLUSTRATIVE CONTENT".
SPACING REVISION following owner feedback: the previous full-page boards felt too crowded. Reduce actual interface density, not merely the scale of the mockup.
Generate one wide landscape 3:2 board containing ONLY ONE large desktop homepage viewport, front-facing, using almost the entire board. A normal-scale TOP-OF-PAGE VIEW, never a full-page overview, no mobile companion, no component specimen sheet or collage. Small outer caption "05 / HOME — MORE SPACE".
Layout priority: luxurious empty space, one clear focal image, a quiet visual rhythm. Generous outer gutters of about 7 percent; large vertical gaps between sections; thin unobtrusive navigation with ample gaps; restrained headline size. Avoid crowding text against image edges. No multiple horizontal rule boxes or heavy borders around sections.
Header: one-line heavy condensed "NIGHT IN KAMPALA" wordmark, navigation exactly "Shop", "Drops", "Our process", "Lookbook", "Events", small "UG / UGX" and search/bag outline icons. A single airy row, no announcement bar, no second row of utilities. Keep all five links clearly readable.
Hero: spacious asymmetric split: a short text area and one original campaign photograph. Only headline "A SECOND LIFE.", short serif subline "Reworked in Kampala." and a modest rectangular "SHOP THE DROP" button. The heading should be commanding without filling every inch. Show ONE young adult Black East African model in reworked denim, styling with a simple printed tee; subtle original brushwork and credible cloth texture. Background simple and softly out of focus, no audience, no extra inset images.
After a generous gap, only the next section starts: modest heading "THE LATEST PIECES" and quiet "View all" link. THREE large product image tiles with wide gutters, no outer card border, isolated on fog grey. Show a washed black tee "Reworked graphic tee" / "UGX 85,000", trousers "Painted denim" / "UGX 160,000", jacket "Reworked jacket" / "UGX 220,000". Product images and captions may approach the bottom viewport edge as a natural scroll crop. Use only one "ONE OF ONE" label per card in small text, no stacked badges.
The process story, events, newsletter and footer sit farther down the page OUTSIDE this viewport; do not squeeze them into this board. This is a normal scrollable homepage, not a miniature rendering of an entire long site.
Readability and spelling are critical. Do not invent dates, event names, people names, venues, impact claims, testimonials or third-party logos. Everything illustrative.
Theme: white header and white product section; near-black hero with white headline and subline. The single campaign photo shows a quiet Kampala street at night under warm streetlight, natural deep shadows, simple softly blurred background. One tiny amber arrow may accompany the hero button; no amber large fills. Night story, day shop. Keep the empty dark areas calm and open.
```
