## Working decisions

- Guest requests use a name and valid phone or WhatsApp contact, with optional email. Confirm collection, delivery, manual payment and any international quote with the customer. Show the item subtotal and **Delivery and final total to be confirmed** when a charge is unknown. Keep provisional collection, delivery and manual-payment wording together in owner-editable settings and mark it for owner review.
- Available and Requested real pieces can receive requests. The bag and initial request create no allocation. Confirming allocates stock per piece. A confirmed piece stays Reserved until an authorized staff member explicitly records Sold or performs an authorized release. Recording payment alone leaves Reserved unchanged.
- Launch uses staff dashboard alerts while the dashboard is open. Unread persists across reload, and opening an order does not mark it contacted. A WhatsApp handoff opens a prefilled chat and requires the shopper to press Send. Hide all WhatsApp order buttons until the business number is saved in settings.
- Keep customer data and request accounting private. Public projections carry availability and safe size labels. The order reference is for conversation and staff lookup. A retry must prove the original submission identity before the server returns its permitted summary.
- Use 24 hours as the approved, editable overdue default. It flags pending work and never releases stock or cancels an order. A timed confirmed hold needs an explicit owner decision. Defaults for submissions are three open requests per normalized phone, one active request per piece and 50 new submissions per source per rolling hour.
